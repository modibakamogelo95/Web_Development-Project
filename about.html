About Leap Sports Co.

Built by athletes, for athletes just starting out.

From one running club to a community-trusted supplier of everyday sports gear.

Our History

From one club to the whole community.

Leap Sports Co. was founded in 2015, starting out by fitting a single local running club with footwear and apparel. As demand grew beyond the club, so did the range — today we supply amateur athletes and fitness enthusiasts across the area with footwear, apparel, and training accessories.

We've stayed independent and locally owned throughout, which means every product on our shelves has been picked with our own community in mind.

  1. 2015 Leap Sports Co. founded, supplying a single local running club.
  2. 2018 Range expands beyond footwear into apparel and accessories.
  3. 2022 Grows into a trusted supplier for amateur athletes community-wide.
  4. 2026 Leap Sports Co. goes online for the first time.
